The process

From report to reunion

Three steps, the same ones every case goes through — whether it's a cat gone overnight or a dog missing for a week.

01 — FILE

File the report

Upload a photo, drop a pin on the last-seen spot, add a description. Takes about two minutes, and starts a live case.

02 — DISPATCH

We go out

Our search & track team — dogs and drone both — is dispatched to search for the missing pet.

03 — REUNITE

We bring them home

Found pets come straight back to you. If no owner turns up after a holding period, they move to our adoption list.
